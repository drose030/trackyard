How the difficulty levels work

Trackyard detects Expert from the audio. Hard, Medium and Easy are built from Expert by leaving notes out, so Expert is the source and can't be regenerated itself.

Fix Expert first, then use Regenerate… to rebuild the easier levels. That replaces hand edits on the levels you regenerate.
